# 🔑 Officer Login & Password Reset Guide

## 1. Logging Into Janata CBS Workbench
1. Open your browser and navigate to the application URL.
2. Enter your assigned **User ID** and **Password**.
3. Select your active **Branch Code** from the dropdown menu.
4. Click **Sign In**.

---

## 2. Mandatory First-Time Password Reset
If your account has temporary credentials or your password has expired:
1. You will be automatically redirected to the **Password Change** screen (`SC.CHANGE.PASS`).
2. Enter your **Current Password**.
3. Enter your **New Password** (Must contain at least 8 characters, uppercase letters, and digits).
4. Confirm your **New Password** and click **Update Password**.
5. Once updated, you will be logged into your officer dashboard.
