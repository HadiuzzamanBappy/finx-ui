# 🌙 Close of Business (COB) Overview

## 1. What is Close of Business (COB)?
Close of Business (COB) is the daily automated processing phase where general ledger balances are calculated, interest posting is executed, and system daily logs are archived.

---

## 2. Teller Guidelines During COB
- Tellers MUST log off active transaction screens before COB execution begins.
- Check the **COB Registry Status** screen (`SC.COB.REGISTRY`) to verify system status.
